ONE EXTENSION. TWO WAYS TO RUN IT.

Choose Local
or Cloud.

Change modes in extension Settings.

01 / LOCAL

On your computer.

Free and open source. No PigeonBox account. Use a local model, Chrome’s built-in model, Ollama, or your own provider key.

Gmail→PigeonBox→Your device or chosen provider
Explore Local ↗Free forever
02 / PIGEONBOX CLOUD

Skip the setup.

Hosted AI and tracking. No model download, personal AI key, or separate tracker.

Gmail→PigeonBox→Cloud + inference provider
Explore Cloud ↗Subscription

YOUR DATA, BY MODE

Know where
your mail goes.

Local keeps your inbox index in this browser. AI requests go to your device, chosen provider, or PigeonBox Cloud based on your settings. Tracking stores message details and activity—not message bodies.
