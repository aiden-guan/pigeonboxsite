PigeonBox Cloud

The PigeonBox extension runs on your computer for free, with no account. PigeonBox Cloud is the hosted option: no model downloads, no personal API keys, and hosted open tracking.

Get started

Install PigeonBox, then choose PigeonBox Cloud under Settings → How should PigeonBox run? You can also sign in here to manage your subscription.
