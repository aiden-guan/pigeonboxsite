03 — Choose how it runsSwitch in Settings

One extension.
Two ways to run.

Local works in the Gmail tab you have open. Cloud adds hosted AI; connect Google to keep Gmail syncing in the background.

Local

Free · Open source

Yours. On your machine.

  • →Free and open source. No PigeonBox account.
  • →Runs in your open Gmail tab; no Gmail API OAuth.
  • →Index, settings, drafts and downloaded models stay in this browser.
  • →Choose on-device AI, Ollama or your own key.
  • →Optional tracking on a server you host.
Install Local ↗About Local →

Cloud

Early access / Beta

Hosted. Keeps working after Gmail closes.

  • →Hosted AI, thread analysis and Focus Queue are live in beta.
  • →Connect Google for background Gmail sync.
  • →Prepared drafts, synced Ask and managed tracking are still testing.
  • →Mail bodies aren’t logged or stored by default; summaries are encrypted.
