Common questions

Is Local a trial?

No. Local is the full open-source extension, with no PigeonBox account or subscription.

Can I cancel Cloud?

Yes. Manage the subscription from your account’s Stripe billing portal. The extension can run in Local mode regardless of your plan.

What happens to data after account deletion?

Account deletion cancels the subscription and removes hosted tracking data and usage records. Local browser data remains on your device.

Does Cloud store my inbox?

Your inbox index stays in your browser. Cloud processes the content needed for a request and does not store raw email bodies. Hosted tracking stores message metadata and events.

Does PigeonBox use the Gmail API?

No. The extension works in Gmail using InboxSDK and a DOM fallback. Gmail changes can affect that integration.

Does Local send my mail to PigeonBox servers?

No. With a local model, AI content stays on your device. If you choose a provider using your own key, that provider receives the requests you send to it. Optional self-hosted tracking uses the endpoint you configure.

Do I need a powerful computer?

Not for the whole product. Rules and on-device heuristics work with AI off. Smaller local models may produce shorter, simpler drafts; Ollama and your own provider are alternatives.

Can I use Ollama or my own provider?

Yes. Both are supported Local choices. Some choices require a network or a model running elsewhere.

What is Convex used for?

Convex is an optional self-hosted persistent tracker. Local inbox features do not need it, and PigeonBox Cloud does not use it.

Is PigeonBox actually open source?

Yes. The extension is MIT licensed in the public GitHub repository. The hosted Cloud infrastructure is separate.

How reliable is open tracking?

It is a signal, not a read receipt. Blocking and mail privacy proxies can hide or trigger opens. PigeonBox reports “Open detected.”

Does PigeonBox send email automatically?

No. It places draft text in Gmail’s composer for you to review and send.
