PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE

Know where
your mail goes.

The extension runs in Gmail. What leaves your browser depends on the run mode and provider you choose.

LOCAL MODEL

On this device.

Inbox text, index, embeddings, drafts, rules, and settings live in your browser. With an on-device model, AI requests are processed there. If you choose Ollama or a provider with your own key, requests go to that endpoint instead.

PIGEONBOX CLOUD

Processed, not archived.

Email content needed for Cloud summaries, categorization, drafts, rewrites, and Ask Inbox is sent over TLS to PigeonBox Cloud and its configured AI provider. PigeonBox Cloud does not store raw content or put it in operational logs. The local inbox index does not move to Cloud.

What Cloud stores

Usage records contain operation, model, token counts, time, and status, without email content. Hosted tracking stores subject, sender, recipients, Gmail IDs, send time, and open/click events with user agent, a salted IP hash, and classification. It does not store message bodies. Accounts store the sign-in ID and email plus Stripe subscription status; Stripe handles payment details.

Deleting Cloud data

The Account page can delete the Cloud account, hosted tracking data, and usage records and cancel the subscription. Browser data remains on your device and can be cleared in extension Settings.

This page describes the current architecture. A formal privacy notice still requires legal review before commercial launch.
