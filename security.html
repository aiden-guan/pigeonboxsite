Security

  • Sign-in uses OAuth with PKCE. The extension never receives your identity provider's keys, and content scripts in Gmail never receive PigeonBox tokens.
  • Every request is authenticated; the server derives your account from the verified token and never trusts an account ID sent by a client.
  • Data is isolated per account in Postgres, with row-level security as an additional layer.
  • Stripe webhooks are signature-verified and processed once per event.
  • Email content in AI requests is not stored, not logged, and not cached by our AI gateway.

Report a vulnerability privately to the address listed in the PigeonBox repository's SECURITY.md.
